Privacy Policy and account data

Easy Memorization: Memorize the Quran (Elifa Mushaf) · Developer: ELIFA COLLECTIVE TEKSTIL TASARIM TICARET LIMITED SIRKETI · 19 October 2026 · An account is optional.

How can I delete my account and data?

Optional account

No account is needed to read or listen to the Quran. If you sign up, Firebase Authentication processes your email and sign-in details. Google or Apple may share account information when used to sign in. Your password is not added to notes, reading backups or usage statistics.

You may choose a display name or nickname and optionally add your age or date of birth, and gender. This profile is stored separately under your account in Cloud Firestore. Email, age, birth date and gender are not shown to other khatm participants. The name or nickname you choose when joining appears beside your juz.

Device records and cloud backups

Notes, marks, bookmarks, memorization progress, your reading position and settings are stored on your device. Creating an account does not automatically send them to a server. If you separately enable synchronization on the account screen, those records are stored in Cloud Firestore under your account and synchronized between your devices. If the same record changes on two devices, you choose which version to keep. Offline changes stay on your device and synchronization is retried when the connection returns. Turning synchronization off does not delete records on your device. You can also create a dated recovery backup with “Create a new cloud backup.” Synchronized data and backups are not end-to-end encrypted; the service infrastructure processes them. Downloaded audio files and Together administrator keys are not sent.

Each account can hold up to 20 dated cloud backups; the current synchronized record is stored separately. A backup remains in the app until you delete it or delete your account. The infrastructure's disaster-recovery copies follow its own retention policies. When you enable synchronization on a new device, device and cloud records are merged. If you manually restore an older backup, the current device records change; a recovery copy of the previous records is kept on that device.

Account and guest records are kept separately on the device. Copying earlier guest studies into an account is a separate choice. Signing out does not erase device copies; this separation does not encrypt device storage. Take care on shared devices. Clearing app data may remove local records.

Deleting your account and data

Go to Settings → My account → Delete my account. After you authenticate again, your app Firebase account, private profile, synchronized records and cloud backups are deleted. Your Google or Apple account itself is not deleted. You can also delete individual cloud backups in My account.

If you uninstalled the app or cannot access your account, email info@elifaplatform.com with the subject “Elifa Mushaf account deletion request.” Include the email address registered to your account so we can find it; do not send your password. After verifying that you own the account, we delete the account and its associated cloud backups.

Deleting your account does not erase khatm progress or device records. Account links are removed from khatms, and display names on participation linked to that account are anonymized. Save your private return and administrator links first. Clear device app data to remove local records. You may email our contact address with the group code to request removal of Together participation.

Together

The khatm title, name or nickname entered when joining, and juz status are stored on the server. Public khatms are listed in the app; link-only khatms are visible to people who know the link or code. Names and contribution amounts in older shared dhikr records remain stored.

A participation link does not let someone change another person's juz. You manage your own juz using its linked account or private access key; the server stores only a hash of that key. The administrator link is separate and private. Linking guest participation to an account requires a separate choice. Clearing device data may lose your access; save your private return link and do not share it.

Completed groups may be archived 7 days after their last activity, and other groups after 60 days, and removed from public listings. This maintenance does not delete khatm records, reading status or private access links; existing links retain access. Archived groups do not accept new juz claims until an administrator reopens them.

Audio, statistics and service providers

Recitations and related content are obtained from QuranCDN and other source servers. Those servers may see your IP address when your device requests content. Account features use Firebase Authentication; cloud records use Cloud Firestore. Hosting and these services may keep technical logs.

Existing Firebase / Google Analytics measurements in the app may process usage data such as screens and features used, session, language and device. The new account feature does not send email addresses, names, passwords or note content to those measurements. We do not show ads or sell data for advertising.

Permissions and children

Account and backup features do not request access to your precise location, contacts or photos. Reading without an account is open to everyone. Children creating an account or sending personal records to the cloud should be considered under parental supervision.

Contact

For requests to access, correct or delete your data, or for questions, email info@elifaplatform.com.
